# Understand the Role of Cilium in Kubernetes Environments

This section covers understand the Role of Cilium in Kubernetes Environments. It develops cilium's Role In Kubernetes Networking through mechanisms, examples, prerequisites and failure reasoning.

Read the module goals, teaching and closing summary in order. YAML checks are indexed separately and appear after designated summaries. Larger domains include an intermediate checkpoint before the final review.

## Learning Outcomes

- Explain CNI responsibility and Cilium endpoint management.
- Distinguish Pod routing, Service translation and policy enforcement.
- Relate identity and observability to the networking implementation.
- Evaluate optional feature prerequisites rather than assuming every feature is enabled.
